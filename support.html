Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an internet connection to play?

No. Juwa Vault is a fully offline game. Once it's installed, you can play anywhere — no Wi-Fi, no data, no account required.

Is my data collected or shared?

No personal information is collected, stored on our servers, or shared with anyone. Your high score and settings stay on your device. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

The game won't open or keeps crashing. What do I do?

First, try closing the app fully and reopening it, then restart your device. Make sure you're on the latest version from the store and that your device has a little free storage. If it still won't behave, email us with your device model and OS version and we'll dig in.

I lost my high score after reinstalling.

Because everything is stored locally on your device for privacy, scores are not backed up to a server. Uninstalling the game removes its saved data, so a fresh install starts from zero.

How do I report a bug or suggest a feature?

We'd love to hear it. Send us an email with as much detail as you can — a screenshot or short description of what happened goes a long way.
